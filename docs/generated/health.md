# Project health

Measured from the database, following the [CHAOSS](https://chaoss.community) metrics for project health: responsiveness (time to first response, change request
closure ratio), contributor sustainability (contributor absence factor) and release frequency. **Bots are excluded from every response number.** These figures describe
activity. They cannot tell a busy maintainer from one who has stepped back, and they say nothing about quality; read them next to the [register](register.md).
Only aggregates are shown: no person other than the configured maintainers is singled out.
