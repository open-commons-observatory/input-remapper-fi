# The maintainer has largely stepped back; pull-request review is the one channel he still uses

`risk` · status `open` · 2 evidence items · [Back to the register](../register.md)

Issue #853, opened by the maintainer in February 2024, says issues are piling up, that he moved on to other hobbies some time ago, that the co-maintainer is busy elsewhere too, and that he still reviews pull requests but nobody should expect more. In March 2025 he said he had worked on the project a lot since the previous autumn after a serious bug report, hoped that was temporary, and no longer uses the tool himself. In March 2026 he said he very rarely reads issues now, mostly checks that recent commits broke nothing, only occasionally fixes a reproducible bug, is not motivated to build feature requests, and handles packager emails and critical bugs when he finds time, typically in winter. The 2021 contribution guide (#177) already said he would not develop much new after 2.0. The health page shows the same shift in numbers. The numbers alone cannot separate busy from gone; his own words say he still merges pull requests and does little else. Consequence for contributors: a proposal has to be small, tested and self-explanatory.
