# Nobody is triaging the open issue backlog

`improvement` · status `open` · 1 evidence items · [Back to the register](../register.md)

The register and health pages show hundreds of open issues, a growing share of new issues that never get a reply, and Discussions left unanswered. The maintainer thanked them but doubted a clean backlog would change much for him, and said he could not promise to honor the effort; he also mentioned a private planning board that others cannot open. Whether the offer was taken up is not visible. A triage helper is a way to help without touching the code, but only if the maintainer agrees to give them the rights to label and close.
